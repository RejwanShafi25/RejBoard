Write

Solve

Every step, checked

Solve equations and systems, differentiate, integrate, simplify, expand, factor and take limits. The working is written out, every answer is verified before it is shown, and you can drop the result straight onto your board.

Plot

See the curve, move the slider

Plot y = f(x), implicit, polar and parametric curves. Letters like a become sliders, intersections are labelled, and the graph sits on your board and exports with your notes.

FAQ

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. RejBoard is MIT licensed, with no account, subscription or paid tier.

Does the math work without internet?

Yes. The equation solver and graph plotter run on your device and never call a web service.

Where are my boards stored?

On your computer, inside the app. You can also save boards as .rjwb files, or back them up to a folder inside OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or Nextcloud.

Will it run on an old computer?

That is the goal. Drawing, scrolling and saving are tuned for crowded boards and slower machines. The numbers on this page come from a synthetic test board, so real results vary.

Can I use it on a phone or tablet?

Open the web app in your browser and add it to your home screen. It works offline once installed.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub.
